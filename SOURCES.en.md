## Key
- Satoshi Tomokiyo, *Henry III's Cipher with Ambassadors* (Cryptiana)
  - https://cryptiana.web.fc2.com/code/henryiii.htm (Last-Modified 23 Sep 2023)
  - The section on Mayenne-Forget's Cipher-2 lists ff. 218-219 among its uses: « This cipher was used not only by Forget but also by Matignon (ff.218-219, undeciphered) ».
  - Table image: https://cryptiana.web.fc2.com/code/henryiii_MayenneForget2.png (not included here; the `table` values in `data/key.tsv` are this table rewritten with sign names).
