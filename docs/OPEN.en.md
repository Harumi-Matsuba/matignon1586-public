## Signs easily confused
- f (barred, s) / long f (i) / ƒ (n): chosen by context, even within one word.
- The hooked ʃ family: J (s), rh (r), Th (tout).
- ∝ (t) and ∝ with a loop below (en): t in ledict, quant, auantaige; en in qui en auoit, en garde, il y en eust.
- Round ℒ: a in necessaire, faire, fault (written A< in the transcription); n in enployer, reconois (Ln). Burford's key also has both a and n.
- ɍ-shape: p fits in perdirent and aussy perdu, s in meilleurs and hommes.
- 4H (e) and # (r): the e of necessaire and medecins looks like # and is written 4H?.
- # and barred tt (les); ϖ with two humps (il) and three (est): confusions found in the calibration. Two places in f. 218r were corrected; others may remain.
- χ° (q in the key) and the barred h (armée in the key): in the calibration lines they stand where the decipherment has « pour » and « tous ». The places in this letter (χ° in f. 218v line 9, .χ°. in f. 219r line 1) have not been rechecked.
